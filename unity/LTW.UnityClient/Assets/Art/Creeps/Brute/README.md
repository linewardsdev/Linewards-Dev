# Brute Creep Art Brief

Concept: armored ward golem or pressure core.

Read target:

- Wide, heavy, rounded or armored silhouette.
- Reads as health pressure.
- Uses larger plates, a visible core, and slow weighty motion.
- Can show low-health damage through cracked/dim armor elements.

Avoid protected faction silhouettes, bulky medieval creature styling, or medieval armor styling.
