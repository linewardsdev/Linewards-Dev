# Line Wards Branding Guide

## Brand Name

**Line Wards**

## Descriptor

**Competitive Tower Wars**

Use the descriptor to explain the game category and its lineage. It is supporting copy, not part of the core logo mark.

## Brand Position

Line Wards is a fast, readable, mobile-first competitive tower-defense game. Players build defenses, send pressure around a carousel of opponents, and balance immediate survival against long-term economy.

The name is an independent homage to the Warcraft III custom-game tradition of Line Tower Wars. Do not imply affiliation with Blizzard Entertainment, Warcraft, or an official Line Tower Wars release.

## Voice

- Competitive, direct, and strategically confident.
- Clever rather than grim.
- Mobile-readable and welcoming to new players.
- Avoid military realism, gambling language, and pay-to-win framing.

## Logo Direction

- Primary wordmark: `LINE WARDS` in a bold, compact, geometric display face.
- Supporting descriptor: `COMPETITIVE TOWER WARS` in a smaller, clean sans-serif.
- Symbol motif: three linked lanes or ward-towers forming a subtle carousel/triangular loop.
- Prioritize readability at app-icon, phone-header, and store-thumbnail sizes.
- Keep the wordmark usable in one color for small placements.

## Color System

| Role | Color | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Night ink | Deep navy | `#10182F` |
| Arcane blue | Primary energy | `#4DA3FF` |
| Ward violet | Secondary energy | `#9B6CFF` |
| Signal gold | Economy/highlight | `#FFC84A` |
| Mint signal | Success/valid placement | `#59E1B6` |
| Cloud | High-contrast text | `#F4F7FF` |

Use dark navy as the primary field, blue/violet for ward energy, and gold sparingly for emphasis. Do not use red/green as the only way to communicate game state.

## Typography

- Display: wide, blocky geometric sans with slightly softened corners.
- UI/body: highly legible sans-serif with clear numerals.
- Use all caps for the logo and compact action labels; use sentence case for explanatory copy.

## Example Copy

- `Build. Send. Outlast.`
- `Defend your line. Break theirs.`
- `Competitive Tower Wars.`

## Usage Rules

- Keep the descriptor on a separate line below the title.
- Preserve generous empty space around the logo.
- Avoid tiny subtitle text in app icons.
- Do not use copyrighted Warcraft or Blizzard artwork, names, or logos.
- Do not promise live multiplayer, ranked play, or cosmetic purchases until those features are actually available.

## Initial Asset Set

1. Primary landscape wordmark with descriptor.
2. Square app icon: ward-tower/carousel symbol only.
3. One-color wordmark for small UI and social use.
4. Store/header art using the navy, blue, violet, and gold palette.
