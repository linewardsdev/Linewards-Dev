# Proof Of Method Review: Source-Kit Promotion V01

Date: 2026-07-15

## Scope

This note documents the current non-AI proof-of-method slice that informed the AI-assisted pipeline. Arrow tower and Runner creep were promoted from placeholder-dominant visuals toward stronger authored/source-kit silhouettes before any AI source plates are generated.

## Roles Reviewed

### Arrow Tower

- Runtime prefab: `unity/LTW.UnityClient/Assets/Prefabs/Towers/Tower_Arrow.prefab`
- Source motif: `Musket1_2_1`
- Intended read: focused rail / lens / bolt emitter.
- Verdict: useful proof that a long-axis readable silhouette improves the role more than small decorative accents.
- Pipeline implication: AI Arrow candidates should prioritize one dominant rail/lens body over complex ornament.

### Runner Creep

- Runtime prefab: `unity/LTW.UnityClient/Assets/Prefabs/Creeps/Creep_Runner.prefab`
- Source motif: `Dagger4_1_3`
- Intended read: sharp fast dart / spine pressure construct.
- Verdict: useful proof that a single strong dart/spine survives gameplay camera distance better than many tiny details.
- Pipeline implication: AI Runner candidates should keep the silhouette low, narrow, and directional.

## Decision

Proceed with the controlled 2.5D token pipeline for the next art pass, starting with Arrow and Runner contact sheets. Do not replace the source-kit proof assets until generated candidates clearly beat them in phone-scale and grayscale review.
