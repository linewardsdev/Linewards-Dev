Risks worth naming

1. Week 1 finds something structural likely

This is the intended outcome — far better in week 1 than week 4 — but it can eat days.

Mitigate by holding week 2 loosely, not by skipping the play session to protect the schedule.

2. Audio is underestimated most likely to slip the date

Least-tracked P0 item, in no checklist, and the only one with no scaffolding beyond code hooks.

Mitigate by using licensed library audio rather than commissioning original work.

3. First store submission is rejected common

Usual first-timer causes: missing privacy policy, incomplete age rating, placeholder metadata.

Mitigate by completing store assets in week 3, not week 4.

What “done” means on 31 August

  • Runs on iOS and Android phones, with a real name, icon and identity
  • Has music and sound effects
  • Has a title screen, teaches the player how to maze, and reports its own crashes
  • Assets carry normal maps and AO, rendering through the intended post-processing
  • Is on TestFlight and the Play internal track, with real testers giving feedback
  • Is free — no store screen, no payment code. Monetization follows separately

Out of scope: monetization (shipping free first), multiplayer, the match server, accounts and entitlements, graphics Waves 2–3 beyond what Tier B needs.
